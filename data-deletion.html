Data Deletion

Deep Thaw: Ball Drop Puzzle — Puzzle Game for Android

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Deep Thaw has no account. We never ask you to register, we run no servers, and we hold no copy of your game data. Your progress lives on your own device — Section 1 explains how to erase it. Advertising, analytics and crash data is a separate matter, held by Google; Section 2 covers it.

1. Data on Your Device

Your progress, your settings and the record of your attempts at each level are stored in the App's private storage. To remove them:

  • Reset your progress in the game — Settings → RESET PROGRESS. This clears your stars, points, boosters and tutorial state. Your settings and the record of your attempts stay.
  • Clear all App data — Android Settings → Apps → Deep Thaw → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall the App. This removes everything, including your settings, the record of your attempts and your consent choice.

Nothing of this is stored anywhere else: there is no cloud copy to delete, and the App's opt-out from Android's cloud backup keeps it out of your Google Drive.

2. Advertising, Analytics and Crash Data

To reset or delete your advertising identifier, open Android's settings — on most devices under Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads. Where the App asks for consent, you can withdraw it at any time: reopen the dialog through the privacy options in the App's Settings screen.

Withdrawing stops personalized advertising and stops analytics and advertising identifiers being read from or written to your device. It does not switch measurement off completely: the App keeps sending Google a small aggregated signal that carries no identifier and cannot be tied back to you. We say so plainly rather than promise a silence we do not deliver — the legal basis for that reduced measurement is explained in Section 6 of the Privacy Policy. Crash reporting is not behind the consent dialog at all, for the reason given in Section 5 of the same Policy.

Data already collected through these services sits on Google's systems and carries a randomly generated identifier rather than your name or account. We cannot find your records from your name or your email address. If you are able to supply your app-instance identifier, we can ask Google to delete the analytics events recorded against it. Crash reports are deleted on Firebase's own published schedule.

3. Requesting Deletion by Email

Write to support-komandakycto@proton.me with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will delete what we hold that relates to you, except anything we are required to keep by law. We aim to answer within one month, or sooner where the law that applies to you requires it. Please do not send identity documents unless we ask for something we reasonably need in order to verify your request.
